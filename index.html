We build embodied intelligence that understands people.

Soul Research is a lab developing physical AI that reads the emotional and social context of a room, acts with care, and stays safe around the people it works beside.

What we do

Most embodied AI is built to finish a task. We build it to be present with people.

Embodied intelligence, machines that see, move, and act in the physical world, is arriving fast. The hard part is no longer motion. It is judgment: knowing when to yield, when to ask, when to wait, and how to remain predictable when the world around it is not.

We study the systems that give a machine that judgment. Perception that recognizes hesitation, attention, and distress. Policies that treat human comfort as a first-class objective. Behavior that is interruptible, legible, and safe by construction rather than safe by patch.

At the center is a world model that includes people. Not just objects and obstacles, but intentions, attention, and emotional state, so the system can predict how a moment will unfold and choose the action a careful person would.

We call the quality we are after a soul: not mysticism, but the interior model of other people that lets a system act with genuine care.

Empathy

Systems that model the emotional and social state of the people around them, and let that understanding shape every movement and decision.

Safety

Embodied agents that are predictable, interruptible, and aligned with human intent, even under uncertainty, even when something goes wrong.

Embodiment

Intelligence grounded in the physical world: perception, manipulation, and movement learned from real interaction, not text alone.

Approach

A machine that truly understands people is safer to be near.
