A research lab

We build embodied intelligence that understands people.

Physical AI that reads the emotional and social context of a room, acts with care, and stays safe around the people it works beside.

Research

Most embodied AI is built to finish a task. We build it to be present with people.

Embodied intelligence is arriving fast: machines that see, move, and act in the physical world. The hard part is no longer motion. It is judgment: knowing when to yield, when to ask, when to wait, and how to remain predictable when the world around it is not.

We study the systems that give a machine that judgment. Perception that recognizes hesitation, attention, and distress. Policies that treat human comfort as a first-class objective. Behavior that is interruptible, legible, and safe by construction rather than safe by patch.

At the center is a world model that includes people. Not just objects and obstacles, but intentions, attention, and emotional state, so the system can predict how a moment will unfold and choose the action a careful person would.

We call the quality we are after a soul: not mysticism, but the interior model of other people that lets a system act with genuine care.

Empathy

Model the emotional and social state of the people nearby, and let that understanding shape movement and decision.

Safety

Keep agents predictable, interruptible, and aligned with human intent under uncertainty, including when something goes wrong.

Embodiment

Ground intelligence in the physical world: perception, manipulation, and movement learned from real interaction, not text alone.
